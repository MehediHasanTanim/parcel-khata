# Play Store listing

## English
**Title:** Parcel Khata: Order & COD Tracker
**Short (80):** Track Facebook/WhatsApp orders, courier COD cash, returns and real profit.
**Full:**
Parcel Khata is a simple ledger for online sellers.
- Record orders in seconds; reuse customers, products and couriers.
- Know which parcels are still with the courier and how much COD cash they owe you.
- Reconcile courier payouts: shortfalls, disputes and write-offs, with a full audit trail.
- Handle returns and see real profit per order after courier fees and returns.
- Works offline. Your data stays on your phone; back it up to a file you control.
- Bangla and English. PIN / fingerprint lock.
Manual entry only: it never talks to couriers or social apps and never sends messages by itself.
