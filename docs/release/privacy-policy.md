# Privacy policy: Parcel Khata

_Last updated: 2026-10-15_

Parcel Khata is a record-keeping app for sellers. **Your business data stays on your phone.**

## What the app stores (on your device only)
Orders, customers (names, phone numbers, addresses), products, couriers, payouts, returns, notes and
attachments you add, and app settings. This data is never uploaded to us. We run no server for it and
cannot read it.

## What leaves your device
- **Nothing automatically.** There are no accounts and no sync.
- **Files you choose to share:** backups, CSV exports and PDFs go where you send them (WhatsApp, Drive, email).
  Backups can be protected with a passphrase you set; we cannot recover a lost passphrase.
- **Messages:** when you tap WhatsApp or Call for a customer, your phone's WhatsApp/dialer opens with the
  number and a text you can edit. You press Send; the app never sends messages itself.
- **Fonts:** the first PDF you create may download a font from Google Fonts. No personal data is sent.
- **Optional usage and crash reports:** off unless you turn them on. If enabled they contain event names,
  counts and durations, and scrubbed error text. They never include customer names, phone numbers,
  addresses or amounts.

## Permissions
Notifications (daily digest, optional), biometric unlock (optional), and file access only through the
system file picker/share sheet when you export, import or back up. No contacts, location, camera
or microphone permission is requested.

## Security
App-private storage, optional PIN/biometric lock, hidden previews in the app switcher, no Android cloud
backup, and "Erase all data" in Settings > Security & privacy.

## Your control
Export everything as CSV at any time. Erase everything with one action. Uninstalling the app deletes its data.

## Children
Not directed to children.
