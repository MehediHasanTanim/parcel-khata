# Privacy policy: draft wording for the optional account / sync (not live)

Use only when cloud sync ships. Until then `privacy-policy.md` stays accurate ("data never leaves the
device").

## Optional account and cloud sync
You can create an account with your mobile number and turn on sync. Sync is **off by default** and the
app keeps working fully without it.

When sync is on, the orders, customers, products, couriers, payouts, returns and expenses in the app
are copied to our servers (encrypted in transit; encrypted at rest) so that your devices and any
helpers you invite see the same data. Customers' names, phone numbers and addresses are included
because they are part of your orders. Only people you invite to your shop can read it; other shops
cannot.

We use your mobile number to sign you in (one-time code by SMS). We do not sell your data, show ads, or
use it to train models. Optional diagnostics stay opt-in and never contain customer details.

You can export your data and delete your account (and all server copies) from Settings. Turning sync
off keeps your local data on the device.

## Play Data safety changes when sync ships
- Data collected: contact info (phone number), user-generated content (orders, customers, financial
  records), device or other IDs (device id for sync).
- Shared with third parties: no (service providers acting on our behalf only: hosting, SMS delivery).
- Data encrypted in transit: yes. Users can request deletion: yes (in-app + account deletion).
- Account required: only for sync.
