# Parcel Khata: Privacy Policy

_Draft, review before publishing. Last updated: 2026-10-17._

Parcel Khata is an order and cash-on-delivery tracker for small sellers.

## Your business data stays on your phone
Orders, customers, products, payouts and expenses are stored only on your device. We do not receive
them. Backups you create are files you choose where to save or share (optionally encrypted with your
passphrase).

## Advertising (free version)
The free version shows small banner ads served by **Google AdMob**. To show ads, Google's SDK may
collect and use device and usage information such as your advertising ID, IP address, device type, and
ad interactions, and may store identifiers on your device. We request **non-personalised ads** and
never pass any of your orders, customers, amounts or notes to the ad service. Ads do not appear on
screens that show customer details, money, backups or security settings.

- Where required, you will be asked for consent and can change it later in More > Privacy and ad
  choices.
- You can reset or limit your advertising ID in your phone's settings.
- Google's policy: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

## Camera, Face ID, notifications
The camera is used only to scan a courier barcode (nothing is saved or sent). Face ID/fingerprint is
used only to unlock the app. Notifications are optional daily reminders created on your device.

## Optional diagnostics
Off by default. If you turn it on, crash reports and anonymous usage counts are sent without any
customer names, phone numbers, addresses or amounts.

## Optional Google Drive backup
If you turn it on, an encrypted copy of your backup is stored in a hidden app folder in your own
Google Drive (`drive.appdata` scope). Only this app can read it.

## Your choices
You can erase all data in Settings > Security and privacy, and uninstall the app at any time.
